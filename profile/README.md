# Zorlya

**Jobs from every country we can reach, scored against your resume.**

Zorlya collects roles from every kind of employer: private companies, government and public agencies, state-owned enterprises, banks, universities, nonprofits, international organisations and social enterprises. Every role names its source and links back to the original posting, and every score shows the line of your resume that earned it. Free for job seekers.
