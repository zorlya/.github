## About

Zorlya collects roles from every kind of employer: private companies, government and public agencies, state-owned enterprises, banks, universities, nonprofits, international organisations and social enterprises. Free for job seekers.

Three rules hold for every role we show:

- **It names its source.** You always see where the posting was read from.
- **It links back.** The original posting is one click away, and applying happens there or with an account you control.
- **Its score shows its evidence.** Each score quotes the line of your resume that earned it and names the gap that cost it.

## Where the jobs come from

Zorlya reads public pages only, respects `robots.txt` and each site's terms, and leaves out defence and arms employers. The full list of sources is public: [zorlya.com/sources](https://zorlya.com/sources).
