# Security policy

We take the safety of resumes and accounts seriously. This policy is the default for every repository in the Zorlya organization that has no `SECURITY.md` of its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Write to **[security@zorlya.com](mailto:security@zorlya.com)**. Please do not open a public issue or a pull request for a security problem.

Include, as far as you can:

- what you found and where (the address, the app or the endpoint);
- the steps to see it, or a short proof of concept;
- what it lets someone do, and to whose data;
- how to reach you, and whether you want to be credited.

## What happens next

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| We acknowledge your report | Within two working days |
| We confirm the problem, or tell you why we do not see one | After we have reproduced it |
| We fix it and tell you | Depends on the severity; we keep you informed |

## Please do not

- read, change or delete other people's data to show that a problem is real;
- run denial of service tests, or send spam or phishing through Zorlya;
- share details publicly before we have had a chance to fix the problem.

## What is covered

Anything Zorlya runs: [zorlya.com](https://zorlya.com), [app.zorlya.com](https://app.zorlya.com), [hiring.zorlya.com](https://hiring.zorlya.com) and the MCP server at `mcp.zorlya.com`.

How we handle resumes and accounts is described on [zorlya.com/security](https://zorlya.com/security).
