# Code of conduct

Zorlya is built by people for people looking for work, and many of them are doing it in a hard moment. We keep our own spaces to the same standard: issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and any event or channel where someone speaks for the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Be specific and kind when you review. Say what to change and why.
- Give credit where it is due.
- Respect privacy. Resumes, applications and messages belong to the people who wrote them.
- Say when you do not know something, and ask.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, threats, insults or demeaning comments, in any form.
- Discrimination or jokes about someone's background, identity, gender, age, disability, religion, nationality or appearance.
- Sexual language or attention that is unwelcome.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, and spam.

## Reporting

Write to [conduct@zorlya.com](mailto:conduct@zorlya.com). Include the place, the date and what happened. We read every report, reply within two working days, and keep the reporter's name private unless they agree to share it.

## What happens next

The project owner reviews the report. Depending on what happened, we may ask for a change, remove a comment, or remove someone from our spaces. We tell the people involved what we decided.

## Scope

This applies in every Zorlya space, and wherever a person represents Zorlya in public.
